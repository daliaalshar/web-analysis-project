Responsible for acquiring and preserving the Kingdom's written heritage. Houses thousands of original manuscripts and rare books printed before 1950. Uses specialized technology for digital archiving.

Includes a restoration laboratory using chemical and manual methods to preserve ancient parchments.

Manages the National Collection and processes all books published in Saudi Arabia through the Legal Deposit system. Organizes millions of titles using the Dewey Decimal Classification.

Manages the ISBN records for Saudi publishers.

Collects and indexes daily newspapers, weekly magazines, and academic journals. Maintains a complete archive of Saudi newspapers from their first issues.

Provides a digital indexing service for articles in major Saudi journals.

National archive for academic research. Receives and catalogs Master's and PhD dissertations from all Saudi universities and Saudi students abroad.

Supports Big Data research with a massive repository of academic studies.

Manages historical photographs, documentary films, maps, and sound recordings documenting Saudi geography and history.

Currently migrating analog media into modern digital formats.

Provides infrastructure for remote access to digital content, manages electronic databases, and ensures the library's website is accessible for global researchers.

Uses advanced metadata standards to link KFNL resources with global digital library networks.
