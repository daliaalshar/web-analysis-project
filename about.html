Establishment Date

The King Fahad National Library was established in 1403 AH (1983 AD) in Riyadh to serve as the national library of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, responsible for preserving the country's intellectual production.

Purpose of Establishment

The library was founded to collect, organize, and preserve Saudi intellectual output, and to provide information resources for researchers, students, and knowledge seekers. It also aims to support the cultural and scientific movement in the Kingdom.

Cultural and Scientific Role

The library contributes to supporting scientific research and spreading culture by providing books, references, manuscripts, and academic theses. It also organizes exhibitions and cultural programs, and offers advanced information services for researchers.

Affiliated Authority

The King Fahad National Library is affiliated with the Ministry of Culture in Saudi Arabia and operates within the framework of national cultural institutions aiming to preserve the country’s intellectual heritage.

Library Mission

To collect, organize, and preserve national intellectual production, and provide advanced information services that support scientific research and enhance knowledge in society.

Library Vision

To become a leading national center in knowledge management and intellectual heritage preservation, and a primary source of information and cultural and scientific services in the Kingdom.
