About the Kind Fahd National Library

The King Fahd National Library is the only national library of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and among its objectivesand tasks is the collection,organization, control, documentation, promotion, and dissemination of Saudi national intellectual intellectual output.

Objectives of the King Fahd National Library

  1. Collecting, organizing, and making available national intellectual output.
  2. Providing high-qualiy information services to all user groups using the latest technologies.
  3. Leading and developing the Saudi library and information socience.
  4. Transitioning to e-government services.
  5. Enhancing the libarary's cultural and social role.
  6. Improving the library's performance efficiency.
